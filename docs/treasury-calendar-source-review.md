# Announced Treasury funding calendar supplement

Reviewed October 27, 2026. The existing complete Fiscal Data auction capture ends its results on October 15 and contains no future auction dates. A fresh bounded Fiscal Data query also returned no future auction dates. Its future issue date alone is insufficient for an announced funding calendar. The new independent supplement therefore uses the exact [TreasuryDirect announced-securities API](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/TA_WS/securities/announced?format=json), HTTP 200, rather than inferring a regular auction schedule.

## Identity, reuse and scope

[TreasuryDirect's announcements page](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/auctions/auction-announcements/) identifies offering amount, announcement, auction and issue dates as its forthcoming-auction facts. TreasuryDirect also publishes [security API documentation](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/legal-information/developers/web-api-security/). The captured feed reports competitive auction date in `competitiveAuctionCloseDate`; its midnight string is a date representation, not an imported midnight close or publication time. Only the date is retained. No auction-close or issue/settlement clock is imported.

Commercial scope is the numerical federal announcement facts and noncreative dates, names and instrument classes. The [Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/) excludes facts from protection; [17 USC 101 and 105](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#105) address federal official-duty works. Applying these rules to this narrowly projected Treasury announcement data is the recorded scope judgment. This is not a claim that TreasuryDirect has granted a blanket API license or CC0 dedication, and it does not transfer Fiscal Data's dataset policy to another feed. Credit U.S. Treasury, Bureau of the Fiscal Service, without implying endorsement. Narrative, page layout, imagery, logos, third-party identifiers, quoted securities prices and all unlisted API fields are excluded.

The feed's raw CUSIP fields are neither retained nor used for identity. Identity combines the agency's auction date, normalized instrument class, term and maturity date. Raw bodies are hashed in memory and discarded. The snapshot retains the exact URL, SHA-256, retrieval time and Last-Modified if present. `sourceReceiptIndex` links every event to its response receipt.

## Adapter and admission

`collectTreasuryAuctionCalendar` requests that exact API once, bounded to 4 MiB and 1,000 response rows. It retains only returned records with auction dates from 30 days before through 90 days after the Eastern capture date. These query/filter bounds do **not** establish complete future coverage. All dates must be valid, dates must follow announcement ≤ auction ≤ issue < maturity, and announcement must not be after the capture date. Types, explicit floating/inflation flags, gross offering amounts, reopening and cash-management classes are validated. Duplicate identities and unknown schemas fail closed.

`treasuryAuctionEvents` emits separate auction and issue/settlement events, with `time:null`, `scheduledAt:null` and `scheduleBasis:'announced-date'`. Offering amounts are gross face offerings, including rollovers; they are not net borrowing, accepted awards or settlement cash proceeds. Dates alone never establish that an auction has completed or a result has published. No ordinary calendar pattern, future amount, result or publication timestamp is generated.

`assertTreasuryAuctionCalendar` separately admits the exact projected fields, source, bounds and derived events, rejecting altered clocks, added content and event amounts inconsistent with retained inputs. The build validates this independent supplement. `node scripts/capture-treasury-auction-calendar.mjs` is the narrow refresh command; failed retrieval or admission leaves the prior supplement intact. The original `official-snapshot.js`, complete auction history, historical releases and frozen exports are unchanged.

The actual capture at **2026-10-28T03:03:18.614Z** (October 27 Eastern) retained 42 announcement records and 84 auction/issue events, with individually listed events ending November 3. Future auctions include October 28 17-week bills ($75 billion) and 2-year FRNs ($30 billion), and October 29 4-week bills ($115 billion), 8-week bills ($110 billion) and 7-year notes ($44 billion). This evidence is independent of whether the older results feed has retained their eventual results.

The [official quarterly refunding page](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financing-the-government/quarterly-refunding) is linked as a borrowing/refunding document reference. No future refunding release date or time was verified in this scope, so no dated refunding event is invented.

## Other Treasury workspace changes

Auction UI states are derived at the current Eastern date: future/today announced, elapsed results not retained, incomplete result inputs, or complete verified results. Verification requires the official ratio and correct instrument rate, an offering amount and complete reconciled competitive allocations. These display states do not rewrite stored agency `status` or CSV schemas. Latest reported and latest complete verified result dates are separate; the retained capture has 6,268 complete verified rows, one incomplete/unreconciled row and five elapsed rows without retained results, with latest complete results October 15.

The refinancing monthly ladder sums the existing valid current MSPD profile into 24 calendar-aligned maturity intervals. Cumulative month 12 and month 24 reconcile to existing cumulative horizon totals; zero buckets represent no contractual maturities in a complete profile. FRN resets remain separate from final maturities. The current 12-month exposure is $10,640.16 billion, 33.43% of eligible principal, through September 30, 2027. Scenario headings identify incremental interest-cost sensitivity and retain the existing hypothetical baseline, FRN, TIPS, zero-floor, deficit and rollover assumptions.

Rates comparison shortcuts select actual retained observations: previous observation for 1D, last observation on/before calendar targets for 1W and 1M, and prior year-end for YTD. Missing targets stay unavailable. All selected nominal, real and compensation changes share the same two dates; compensation change reconciles to nominal change minus real change. Official FOMC scheduled dates and documented Treasury methodology dates provide labeled event comparisons without claiming a verified publication instant or causal effect. Latest retained indicators remain a separate section.
