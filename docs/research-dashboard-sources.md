## Failure policy

The publication-history follow-up admits [1,076 exact official GDP publication facts](publication-vintages-source-review.md), 98 quarters from 2002Q1 through 2026Q2, with original cells/tokens/stages/annotations, publication-day evidence, retrieval hashes and BEA reuse attribution. The three original June-workbook records remain unchanged; new facts use the exact September edition. `scripts/publication-policy.mjs` and its row manifest validate retained subsets and complete requested batches; build requires the full target. GDP-not-revised confirmations and the documented 2026 annual update remain distinct from inferred causes. This archive is outside daily freshness. Other measures/editions, earlier vintages and historical supplemental/calendar replay remain deferred.

Blocked or unapproved feeds do not produce substitute observations. Keep source-specific last-good capture times and visible cached/unavailable states. A rights decision for one dataset does not cover unrelated calendars, prose, logos or third-party market data. No new credentials, paid feeds or infrastructure are authorized by this ledger.

The [BEA revision-explanation follow-up](publication-explanations-source-review.md) separately admits 33 publication-specific paraphrases from 29 verified dated releases under FAQ147. Exact identities, prose, source sections, publication days and independent retrieval provenance are enforced by `scripts/publication-explanation-policy.mjs`; the build requires the complete reviewed scope. Routine revisions, first publications, the specific 2023Q1 comprehensive update and 2024Q1/2025Q1/2026Q1 annual updates are distinct. The [three targeted update additions](gdp-quarter-updates-source-review.md) preserve the existing thirty explanation records. Other unreviewed explanations remain unavailable. This does not alter the numerical archive or expand permissions to unrelated BEA content.
