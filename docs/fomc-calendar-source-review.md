# Dated FOMC calendar and official material references

Reviewed 2026-10-22. The [Board FOMC calendar](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm) returned HTTP 200 at the exact keyless endpoint `https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm`. This admission is factual dates, meeting association and links to Board materials. The [Board public-domain/copyright policy](https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm) permits copying and distribution of its information unless otherwise indicated, with source citation. No contrary notice appeared on these Board-authored factual calendar records. The scope excludes seals/logos, third-party materials and wholesale framing of the official website. Display and CSV credit the Board and link the official calendar.

`scripts/sources/calendar-fomc.mjs` reads the current year's listed meeting rows and the following year's rows when published. It preserves meeting start/end dates; projection-associated asterisks; exact dated policy-statement, minutes and projection-table HTML URLs; and the explicit minutes release dates supplied by the calendar. A missing following year yields a coverage warning and no generated dates. A missing current year, changed date structure, duplicate meeting, link/meeting-date mismatch, unrelated document origin or missing explicit minutes release date fails for review.

All events have **day precision**: `time: null`, `scheduledAt: null`, `datePrecision: day`. The source calendar does not specify the release hour, so the collector does not stamp 14:00 Eastern onto its records. It also does not add 21 days to meeting dates to fabricate future minutes events. The calendar states the usual three-week minutes lag, but only explicitly listed minutes dates are admitted. Ordinary meeting date schedules use program `fomc-decision`; explicit minutes dates use `fomc-minutes`. Both map to `policyTargetUpper` / `policyTargetLower` as contextual indicators, not as observed decision results.

Official document references are separate `documents` records with `kind`, `meetingDate`, `documentDate`, `url`, `sourceUrl`, `datePrecision` and a limitation explaining the evidence. A statement's date is encoded in the exact official URL and matched against the meeting end. A minutes URL encodes its meeting date; its **document date** comes from the calendar's explicit release date. Projection-table links are admitted only on projection-associated meetings. The association flag does not generate a numeric projection or a future projection publication record.

The linked decision, minutes and Summary of Economic Projections document **contents have not been captured or parsed** by this collector. Calendar links are consequently described as official references, never verified publication results. No decision quotation, policy reaction function, release instant, SEP median/range, dot distribution, market-implied probability or market forecast is manufactured from a link. Numeric SEP ingestion remains outside this bounded calendar/reference admission: its changing forecast-year horizons, conditional participant population, median/range measures and non-calendar document schema need a separate exact field/period contract before those values could be displayed or exported as observations. The current page provides direct dated official projection references instead.

The live capture retained 16 decision meeting schedules across 2026/2027, six explicitly dated 2026 minutes events, and 15 official document references (six decisions, six minutes, three projection tables). At capture, the next listed decision meeting ended 2026-10-28; the next projection-associated meeting ended 2026-12-09. These remain schedules with day precision, not actual publication claims. The July 2026 meeting minutes list 2026-08-19; the September 2026 meeting minutes list 2026-10-07, keeping meeting and document dates distinct.

The two-MiB bounded response records the exact source URL, SHA-256 and retrieval instant; redirects fail. The parser executes no HTML, loads no linked media and follows no document links. `collectFomcCalendar` returns source ID `fomc`, `events`, `documents`, `coveredYears`, `fetchedAt`, `provenance`, `coverageNote` and warnings. `fomcCalendarPolicy`, `matchesFomcCalendarFeed` and `assertFomcCalendarData(source)` are exported from `scripts/credit-context-policy.mjs` for shared admission. The shared calendar validation, sorting, display and export must preserve day precision, with no fallback UTC timestamp invented for these events. Event IDs are `fomc-decision-YYYY-MM-DD` / `fomc-minutes-YYYY-MM-DD`.

Tests verify day precision, explicitly dated minutes, associated projection references, source/link/date agreement, duplicate rejection, absent future-year coverage and rejection of invented clock fields. Final integration and application-wide tests are recorded by the main implementation.
