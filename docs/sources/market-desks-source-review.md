# Official public specialist market data review

Reviewed 1 November 2026. Adds twelve isolated source families and 59 series; the existing 32 refresh inputs and their historical captures are unchanged. Complete candidate captures, response SHA256 hashes, actual receipt timestamps and source modification dates are retained in `output/stitch-page-audit-2026-11-01/market/market-capture-report.json`. Each candidate is also gzip-compressed for review. No canonical snapshot is changed by this module.

## Interfaces

`src/market-desk-definitions.js` exports `marketDeskDefinitions` (exact per-family native codes) and browser-safe `marketDeskRegistry`. `scripts/sources/market-desks.mjs` exports `marketDeskCollectors`, with separate collectors for `fedQt`, `dealerDetails`, `moneyRates`, `repoOperations`, `ofrRepo`, `ofrMmf`, `nyfedTriParty`, `dkwInflation`, `fedFx`, `ecbFx`, `ecbPolicy`, `fedSep`. The SEP/FX helper is isolated in `market-fed-projections.mjs`.

`validateMarketDeskSource(source)` is the boolean browser admission API; `assertMarketDeskSource(source)` throws a specific error. `assertMarketDeskSources(sources,{complete:false})` accepts a partial source dictionary or snapshot for Node admission. `marketDeskPolicies` and `matchesMarketDesksFeed(url,feed)` supply exact additive policy scope. SEP intentionally has `series:{}` and named projection tables; generic refresh must use the narrow validated tables-only exception for it.

## Admitted contracts

- **H.4.1 / QT:** Same [official release ZIP](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h41/data/FRB_h41_xml.zip) and existing bounded ZIP reader as the core balance sheet. Thirteen exact Wednesday `.WW` level series cover Treasuries, agency debt, MBS, currency, primary/secondary/seasonal credit, central-bank swaps, discontinued BTFP, repo and reverse-repo liability categories. Exact SDMX category/component/frequency/currency/unit attributes are checked. Native USD millions / 1000 gives billions. `NA/-999999` stays null, including the discontinued BTFP tail. Holdings changes are not exact QT runoff, and H.4.1 accounting is not SOMA adjusted par.
- **Dealer details:** [NYFed taxonomy](https://markets.newyorkfed.org/api/pd/list/timeseries.json) and [structural breaks](https://markets.newyorkfed.org/api/pd/list/seriesbreaks.json) are captured before the exact SBN2024 request. Eight buckets cover bills, FRNs, coupon maturities, TIPS and net agency MBS. All net position components may be signed. Grouped buckets require every exact-date input; `*` suppression remains null. No pre-July-2024 taxonomy splice or change-field substitution.
- **Money rates:** Official [TGCR](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/tgcr), BGCR and OBFR API responses preserve effective dates, percent median rates, USD billions of underlying volume, 1/25/75/99 transaction percentiles and revision markers. Reviewed reduced-volume/prior-day contingency flags are preserved; an unreviewed footnote identifier fails closed. Percentiles are not uncertainty intervals and these rates are not forward OIS.
- **Repo operations:** NYFed public results retain separate operation identities, settlement/maturity dates, source notes and each collateral tranche. Multiple Price auctions preserve `minimumBidRate`; Full Allotment operations preserve `percentOfferingRate`. These are separate meanings. Explicit readiness/small-value exercises are excluded. Only regular overnight accepted trade dollars sharing an operation date are summed and divided by one billion. Neither source counterparty counts nor market take-up assumptions are invented.
- **OFR repo:** Eight exact preliminary [OFR-collected repo aggregates](https://www.financialresearch.gov/short-term-funding-monitor/datasets/repo/) cover DVP/GCF weighted mean rates, transaction volume, outstanding volume, and TRIV1 mean/transaction volume excluding Fed transactions. Each full-series response contains its exact mnemonic, name, vintage, frequency, adjustment, native unit and rights field. Rates remain percent; native USD becomes billions. Final and preliminary histories and TRI/TRIV1 are never spliced. Null disclosures remain missing. The current reviewed `disclosure_edits` arrays are empty; a newly structured nonempty edit list fails closed pending review.
- **OFR MMF:** Five [SEC N-MFP/OFR aggregates](https://www.financialresearch.gov/short-term-funding-monitor/datasets/mmf/) supply month-end total, Treasury, agency, repo and Federal Reserve repo investments. Native USD becomes billions. These are monthly reported investments, not daily flows or a substituted daily ON RRP measure. Missing filing dates stay absent.
- **Tri-party:** The [NYFed workbook](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/tri-party-repo) is read through the bounded ZIP helper, checking exact sheet relationship and column headings. Only the November-2025 regime onward is admitted. The [explanatory notes](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/Research/Interactives/Data/tri-party-repo/tripartyrepo_explanatory_notes) distinguish ordinary collateral from separate Sponsored GC and Federal Reserve quantities. The workbook's reported Total includes those separate quantities; ordinary collateral is explicitly calculated as Fedwire-eligible plus non-Fedwire-eligible totals. Monthly seventh-business-day snapshot values are before haircut, not daily volumes. Collateral statistics and observation/repo counts have separate named tables and units.
- **DKW:** This is a **Federal Reserve Board** staff research model, not a New York Fed observed series. The [research catalog](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/economic-research-data.htm) links the actual underscore-named [DKW_updates.csv](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/DKW_updates.csv); the hyphen-named address in the older note returns 404. Twelve outputs preserve 5-year, 10-year and 5-to-10-year expectations, inflation risk, TIPS liquidity and fitted compensation in native percentage points, labeled `model-estimate`. The [method note](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/tips-from-tips-update-and-discussions-20190521.html) defines compensation as expected inflation plus inflation risk premium minus liquidity premium. The CSV carries the current re-estimation note; history can revise. Daily estimates are updated monthly, and pre-TIPS `NA` remains null. Underlying commercial survey or quote inputs are not redistributed.
- **FX and ECB policy:** Exact H.10 JPY per USD (`RXI_N.B.JA`) and ECB EUR-base USD/GBP/JPY reference rates retain source dates and quotation orientation. The ECB deposit facility is an administered percent rate and historical negatives are valid. H.10 business-date observations are published weekly. These are dated references, not live quotes, forwards or cross-currency basis.
- **SEP:** Official FOMC-calendar discovery selects the latest dated accessible projection HTML. Table 1 retains current-vintage medians, central tendencies and ranges. Figure 2 retains anonymous counts per exact 1/8-point rate level and horizon. Horizons have separate participant totals; no identity or cross-year path is inferred. These `official-projection` facts reside in `tables.projections` and `tables.dots`, with explicit source `asOf`, horizon and measurement basis. No market-probability observation is synthesized.

## Reuse and exclusions

Board-authored statistical fields/model outputs retain [Board disclaimer and reuse terms](https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm), source credit and staff-model labels. NYFed items retain original titles, copyright, [conditional redistribution terms](https://www.newyorkfed.org/privacy/termsofuse), downstream conditions and Meridian non-endorsement notices. OFR-created aggregates are admitted under its [legal notices](https://www.financialresearch.gov/legal-notices/), with requested credit; this does not admit separately supplied FICC-originated feeds. ECB statistics retain [usage conditions](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_statistics/governance_and_quality_framework/html/usage_policy.en.html) and [disclaimer](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/services/using-our-site/disclaimer/html/index.en.html).

Public-only unavailable: CME/futures probabilities, OIS and live FX basis/forward quotes, order-book depth, execution/venue shares, current bid-ask or WI quotes, licensed provider indexes and independently supplied FICC sponsored volumes. BoJ statistical content is excluded because advance commercial reproduction permission was not obtained. Scenarios must remain user/model assumptions; observed public rates cannot establish executable term basis or automatic facility triggers.

All twelve actual provider captures passed shared Node/browser admission. Compact test fixtures are source excerpts, not canonical capture replacements. Full candidates retain their actual complete captured observations and original response hashes.
