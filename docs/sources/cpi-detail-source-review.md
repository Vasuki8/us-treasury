# CPI component indexes, weights and published effects

Reviewed 1 November 2026. The `cpiDetail` family is independent of the original BLS and historical CPI publication inputs. It adds 20 exact native component indexes, 20 exact-endpoint growth measures, and the latest dated BLS release Tables 2, 6 and 7. The source's original five response hashes and retrieval timestamps are retained. The complete candidate was captured at `2026-11-01T16:43:23.282Z`; the independent review reparsed those same retained bytes locally, without another API request or a new retrieval timestamp.

## Official scope and rights

The public [BLS v1 API](https://api.bls.gov/publicAPI/v1/timeseries/data/) request contains the 20 fixed CPI-U U.S.-city-average codes below and exactly ten calendar years. One request fits the public 25-series/10-year limits. The exact [CPI series catalog](https://download.bls.gov/pub/time.series/cu/cu.series) must match program, geography, item, adjustment, title, monthly periodicity and each code's native index base. No registered endpoint, account or commercial provider feed is added.

The reviewed categories are Food `SAF1`, Energy `SA0E`, Commodities less food and energy commodities `SACL1E`, Services less energy services `SASLE`, Shelter `SAH1`, Rent of primary residence `SEHA`, Owners' equivalent rent of residences `SEHC`, Services less rent of shelter `SASL2RS`, Medical care `SAM`, and Used cars and trucks `SETA02`. Each is captured with SA prefix `CUSR0000` and NSA prefix `CUUR0000`. Owners' rent and services less rent use **December 1982=100**; the other eight use **1982–84=100**. These bases are explicit registry units and are not interchangeable.

BLS statistics are admitted under its [copyright information](https://www.bls.gov/opub/copyright-information.htm) and [developer terms](https://www.bls.gov/developers/termsOfService.htm), with source attribution and the BLS post-retrieval analysis disclaimer. No official logo, seal or endorsement is implied.

## Measurement contract

Monthly SA growth is `100 × (index / exact prior-calendar-month index − 1)`. Annual NSA growth uses the exact year-ago month. Month-start dates label monthly reference periods; they are not daily prices or verified release dates. Annual-average `M13` records are excluded. Missing endpoints remain null, and no earlier observation replaces a missing latest value. Source index strings and native footnote objects are retained. Index values must be positive or explicitly missing; duplicate dates/series, malformed footnotes and nonmonthly periods fail admission.

[Table 2](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.t02.htm) retains dated relative importance, annual NSA change, monthly NSA change and three successive monthly changes under the release's SA heading. **Some categories are explicitly NSA exceptions** under source footnote 1. The parser retains row footnote IDs/text and `monthlyAdjustment` rather than labeling every displayed monthly field SA. These nested expenditure categories overlap and cannot be summed as independent components.

[Table 6](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.t06.htm) retains published one-month effects and [Table 7](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.t07.htm) retains twelve-month effects. Rates are percent, effects and standard errors are percentage points, and relative importance is percent of CPI at the explicitly printed preceding-month reference period. Table 6 has NSA category exceptions under source footnote 4. Source dashes remain missing, including the All-items effect; they are not zero. Effects are published facts, not relative-importance weights multiplied by rounded inflation rates.

The parser checks the exact CPI-U/U.S.-city-average table identity, full caption, every reviewed column heading and its date horizon, weight-reference month, footnote meaning, numerical row/cell identity, ranges, duplicate IDs and null markers. Only the exact `Special aggregate indexes` section row in Tables 6/7, with its original row ID and one empty six-column cell, is skipped. An ordinary row with missing numerical cells still fails. The section row was the cause of the first collector failure; weakening missing-cell validation was unnecessary.

The three table vintages must match and cannot be later than the capture's coverage end. Native monthly SA/NSA API histories are current revised histories; they do not recreate original publication vintages. The release tables are independently retained facts from the latest release and do not establish a historical monthly effects panel.

## Admission and verification

The browser boolean validator rebuilds every derived observation and all parsed tables from the retained inputs, checks exact index bases, native footnote consistency, source URLs, five SHA256 receipts, strict UTC retrieval timestamps, real calendar dates and the ten-year request scope. First-time source outages with no capture, series, tables or inputs may remain unavailable without invalidating independent source families. Any retained partial or cached capture must pass full admission.

Regression tests were observed failing before the fixes. Thirteen focused checks now pass using bounded excerpts of the actual captured API, catalog and three release tables. They cover the blank section row; monthly/annual header shifts; native base substitution; duplicated API dates; nonpositive indexes; malformed/native footnotes; NSA exceptions; incorrect capture timestamps and coverage dates; exact missing-month/year-ago endpoints; first-outage isolation; and an invalid clock failing before any API call. Tests read their own small checked-in fixtures and do not depend on local `output/` probes. The complete candidate and original response hashes remain in the audit output for root integration; this review did not write the canonical snapshot.

Immutable captures retain the registry's measurement names, formats, precision, definitions, date conventions and comparison classes at collection time. Admission compares regenerated tables and series by exact semantic JSON: object key order is irrelevant, array order and all field values remain significant, and extra or altered series metadata fails. A regression records the real capture through `recordVintages`, replays it with `asKnownOn`, and admits the resulting archive through `assertCommercialData`. The candidate series were rebuilt locally from the original captured inputs after this correction; all five receipt hashes and the final retrieval timestamp were preserved, without another API request.
