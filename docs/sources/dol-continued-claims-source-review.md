# ETA continued-claims source review

Reviewed 2026-10-22. Scope: add `continuedClaims` from the seasonally adjusted national `ContinuedClaims/SA` field already present alongside initial claims in ETA's existing weekly XML export. No new agency, credential, paid source, logo, or data infrastructure is involved.

## Statistical reuse and exact feed

The [DOL public-domain and copyright policy](https://www.dol.gov/general/aboutdol/copyright) permits reproduction and distribution of public-domain federal material without permission, requires avoiding implied affiliation or endorsement, and distinguishes third-party copyrighted content and protected logos. The [current DOL publication-practices page](https://beta.dol.gov/about/notices/website-content-and-publication-practices) also describes the agency-created information's public-domain treatment and encourages attribution. The scoped national ETA statistical compilation contains no identified third-party content; the review judgment permits commercial statistical display and export, credited to the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration, with actual retrieval metadata and without suggesting endorsement. This does not extend to unrelated prose, imagery, or branded assets. The beta copyright subpage returned HTTP 403 during direct review; the current `www.dol.gov` policy above was accessible and verified.

The [official weekly-data form](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/claims.asp) offers national/state data, beginning/ending years, and XML output. The unchanged exact endpoint is `https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/wkclaims/report.asp`. The existing POST uses `level=us`, `strtdate=current UTC year − 2`, `enddate=current UTC year`, `filetype=xml`, and `submit=Submit`. It yields national `r539cyNational` XML with each `weekEnded` row containing `InitialClaims`, `ContinuedClaims`, and other known fields. The addition reads only `ContinuedClaims/SA`; it does not substitute the NSA count, four-week average, insured unemployment rate, or initial count.

## Meaning and timing

The [ETA weekly-data description](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/claims.asp) distinguishes initial claims from continued weeks claimed. Continued claims approximate insured unemployed workers claiming benefits after an initial claim; they do not measure all unemployed people or new layoffs. The [official weekly-claims technical notes](https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/OPA/newsreleases/ui-claims/20240527.pdf) describe the initial/continued definitions, advance ETA 538 reporting, and subsequent ETA 539 revisions. The API-level `sourceIds` value is `ContinuedClaims/SA`; the displayed count is the published SA count divided by 1,000, in thousands of claims, with the original count retained in `sourceValue`. This scaling is documented and does not create a modeled estimate.

The [official release archive](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/claims_arch.asp) describes a weekly Thursday 8:30 a.m. Eastern release with posted holiday exceptions. Observation weeks and release dates are distinct. The [official weekly release](https://www.dol.gov/ui/data.pdf) can contain newer initial-claims weeks than insured-unemployment weeks. Consequently the collector uses each measure's own actual returned Saturday date, never shifts the continued series to match initial claims, and never assigns retrieval or `rundate` as a historical publication timestamp. `publishedAt` remains null. The app's existing separately reviewed release-calendar rule remains the schedule authority; this change does not create historical publication evidence.

## Coverage and parser rules

The original integration retained the latest 52 past returned weeks independently for initial and continued claims. The [1967-onward history follow-up](dol-claims-history-source-review.md) supersedes that retention limit after verifying the full official response and commercial reuse terms. `parseDol` adds `continuedObservations` alongside the existing `observations` and `reportDate`; the collector publishes `series.continuedClaims` with its own `latestObservationDate`, `coverageNote`, source ID, formula, official status, and measure description.

Only leading and trailing missing counts are trimmed. A real zero remains zero; missing interior counts remain explicit null rows. The parser rejects negative/fractional/malformed/unsafe counts and preserves the existing bounded schema, Saturday validation, duplicate checks, and rejection of unsupported entities/declarations. A wholly missing required continued series, including one with no usable value before the cutoff, fails the source request rather than fabricating a current observation or publishing only part of the required family.

Continued-claims coverage text names its own latest observation. If that date trails the collection cutoff by more than 14 days, a separate continued-claims warning is emitted even when initial claims are current. Both measures warn that the historical export may lag the latest advance news release and that reported values can be revised. No additional backfill, interpolation, seasonal recalculation, or forced alignment is performed.

## Verification status

After confirming official reuse terms, one direct read-only national XML request was attempted with the reviewed existing POST fields. The sandbox initially blocked name resolution; the approved network attempt then received HTTP 502 from the official endpoint. No response was stored, no capture was replaced, and no alternate unofficial feed was used. This review therefore records verified official identity/meaning/terms and synthetic parser/collector regressions; a successful live collection remains an integration check before claiming current continued-claims availability. Existing immutable source captures retain their original bytes.
