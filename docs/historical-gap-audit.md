# Retained historical-data gap audit

October 26, 2026. The user requested a review of remaining historical gaps and compatible official repairs while preserving the current UI and colors. Baseline: `ca66435`, with **136 registered measurements, 129,393 observations, 23 statistical sources and 185 immutable captures**. No invalid or duplicate dates were found; cadence flags were confined to the documented Z.1 annual prefix. The audit covers retained reference histories and supplementary source tables; it does not claim every dataset's earliest possible historical coverage or reconstruct uncaptured original releases.

Three independent reviews and a full catalog inventory checked nulls, duplicate/invalid dates, interior reference-period omissions, raw component fields, derived lookbacks and publication evidence. Daily holidays, weekly posted exceptions and native annual prefixes were evaluated against provider conventions rather than filled from a generic calendar. Raw official responses, request clocks and hashes are retained under ignored `tmp/historical-gap-{audit,treasury,labor-macro,credit-activity}/`.
