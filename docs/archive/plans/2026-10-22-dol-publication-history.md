# Archived DOL claims publication history

**Goal:** Add verified 2026 weekly DOL release facts to Publication history while preserving Graphite, the revised XML histories, the latest release context, all immutable captures and existing BEA publication records.

**Design:** A separate append-only DOL publication archive contains the initial and continued advance estimates and their explicitly revised/unrevised prior-week comparisons, keyed by series, reference week, release day and role. Only links actually listed by the official 2026 archive are candidates: 42 releases, January 8–October 22. The archive is loaded only for the selected claims series. Existing publication cutoff, CSV and frozen-package infrastructure is reused with weekly labels and source-specific evidence. Publication-day replay is separate from actual Meridian retrieval replay; missing advance releases or later revisions remain unavailable.

**Sources:** `https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/claims_arch.asp` and its official 2026 selector response; the 42 exact discovered `https://oui.doleta.gov/press/2026/MMDD26.pdf` URLs; DOL's `https://www.dol.gov/general/aboutdol/copyright` policy. Commercial display/export admission covers agency-authored regular UI national seasonally adjusted statistical facts, excluding logos, charts and third-party content. Record the exact source manifest, counts, hashes, dates and parser evidence before canonical capture. No new dependency, credentials, service or hosting.
