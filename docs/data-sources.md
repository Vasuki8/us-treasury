# Commercial data reuse policy

The public Meridian website is intended to earn advertising revenue. Display and CSV redistribution must use datasets whose terms permit commercial reuse. Free access, an API key, or an official-looking domain is insufficient evidence by itself.

Review date: **October 21, 2026**. The twelve original indicators and Treasury par yield curve below fit this policy. Additional research indicators have separate scoped reviews and executable admissions in the [research source ledger](research-dashboard-sources.md). This document does not extend public-domain status to conditionally licensed New York Fed data.

## Attribution and presentation

- Credit the originating agencies and retain source links, series identifiers and actual retrieval timestamps in the app and exports.
- Display the BLS API's required post-retrieval disclaimer clearly on every page and include it in each exported BLS row. Use the last successful retrieval time even when a later refresh fails.
- Distinguish calculations made by this application from agency-published measures. Payroll differences, year-over-year inflation and earnings changes, indexing and unit conversions retain their formulas and input provenance.
- Present Meridian as an independent site. Do not imply agency affiliation, endorsement or sponsorship, including endorsement of advertisements.
- Use plain text agency credits. Agency seals/logos and third-party photographs, illustrations, news, forecasts or commercial market data are outside this review and are not licensed by the statistical data policies above.

## Enforced dataset scope

The second website-review milestone extends the original scope below through separate dataset reviews: [BLS macro families](bls-macro-family-source-review.md), [BEA monthly macro](bea-monthly-macro-source-review.md), [DOL continued claims](dol-continued-claims-source-review.md), and [policy, activity and Census construction](macro-coverage-source-review.md). The last review admits RESCONST bulk statistics and factual New Residential Construction calendar entries separately from the earlier MARTS approval. Exact source IDs, native units, conditional New York Fed rights and Census originating-program metadata are enforced by `scripts/macro-coverage-policy.mjs`, called from the existing admission gate. References below to the original MARTS-only Census scope describe that earlier review, not a blanket approval of other programs.

`scripts/data-policy.mjs` records the reviewed datasets, source identifiers, feed locations, evidence links and review date. Both `npm run build` and `npm run data:refresh` validate the snapshot against it. An unreviewed agency, series, identifier, feed or supporting provenance URL stops the build or snapshot replacement. Calendar records have a separate allowlist for exact official feeds and program-to-indicator mappings; public event rows must match their captured source records and SHA256 provenance. Tests exercise rejection paths as well as the committed snapshot.

This check enforces the recorded scope; it does not detect future changes to a provider's legal terms. Recheck the primary terms before public launch, when changing feeds, and when adding data. Record any material change here and in the policy module. Confirm commercial display, derived use and downloadable redistribution, including any attribution, caching, rate-limit or third-party restrictions, before admitting a new dataset.

The approved statistical DOL feed is the national historical XML export and the approved statistical Census feed is the MARTS bulk ZIP. Other DOL/Census datasets, state claims, unadjusted substitutions and other retail programs require their own review. The statistical datasets' reviews do not automatically license separate calendar content. Calendar ingestion has separate reviews for BLS annual HTML, BEA official ICS, Census annual retail factual fields, DOL's weekly publication rule/exception table and Treasury Fiscal Data's factual Debt to the Penny estimates. Exact feed paths and program mappings are enforced. Census and Treasury calendar approvals do not extend to copied narrative, HTML or imagery.

DOL dates expand an explicit agency-authored recurring rule, not a guessed cadence. Only the upcoming 90 calendar days are expanded, capped at the last posted exception-year horizon. Posted Wednesday exceptions replace the following nominal Thursday. Each event retains its basis, nominal Thursday and source link; source metadata retains the raw rule and parsed exceptions. The policy rejects unsupported bases, altered release clocks, dates beyond coverage, missing exception replacements and invented observation periods. Historical dates are not reconstructed from a current exception list. The schedule page uses EST generically; [DOL's official release embargo](https://www.dol.gov/ui/data.pdf) calls the time Eastern. Use `America/New_York` including DST and retain that interpretation visibly.

Census events use only explicitly displayed annual-calendar dates, clocks and reference periods; hidden obsolete sort values cannot revive suspended/TBA rows. The next-year canonical calendar and its discovered current-year archive are both required after rollover. The [current MARTS release](https://www.census.gov/retail/marts/www/marts_current.pdf) confirms Eastern release times across the October EDT/November EST transition. Its date paragraph is supporting timezone evidence; the HTML calendar remains the ingested event source.

Treasury dated entries come only from its explicitly listed Fiscal Data calendar records for Debt to the Penny. Verify the exact ID, title and dataset path against its official metadata response; retain hashes and retrieval timestamps for both feeds. The [calendar page](https://fiscaldata.treasury.gov/release-calendar/) describes dates/times as estimates. Treasury's own [calendar time component](https://github.com/fedspendingtransparency/fiscal-data/blob/master/src/components/calendar-entries/calendar-entry-time/calendar-entry-time.jsx) identifies the service clocks as UTC. Preserve those inputs, convert display times through `America/New_York`, and label entries `ESTIMATED` beside the title and in the preview. Do not extend the source horizon, fill holidays or derive publication history from its `released` flag. The policy requires the estimate basis and matching UTC/Eastern fields; observation periods remain unknown.

The independent `treasury-yield-timing` capture stores structured factual guidance from Treasury's methodology, with its own hash, retrieval and refresh status. It has no dated events: par yields are usually available by 18:00 Eastern each trading day, subject to delay. Preserve `America/New_York` as a seasonal timezone rather than a fixed EST/UTC offset. The build allowlist requires the exact reviewed fields, qualifications, source URL and yield relationship, and rejects fabricated dates, publication instants, copied narratives or altered programs. Parser scope is the methodology article inside the main content; changed, missing or conflicting timing language fails closed. Retain the last-good rule and original provenance on failure, independently of debt schedules and statistical observations. Calendar and yield detail display ongoing timing and cache status separately from observation retrieval. No trading-day holiday calendar, early-close schedule or exact yield publication history is inferred. The methodology's 3:30 p.m. input quotation clock is not used as release timing.

Calendar records describe scheduled timing, not confirmed publication, results or consensus. Preserve source titles, explicit reference periods, UTC instants and Eastern date/time fields; use current IANA daylight-saving rules. BLS annual calendars can have uneven program horizons (2027 JOLTS dates are absent in the current capture). BEA UID, sequence and update timestamps describe calendar records, not statistical release history. On refresh failure retain the last-good agency schedule with its original retrieval and provenance; mark it cached. Replacing a successful source removes withdrawn entries.

Claims carry a visible historical-feed limitation: the latest available table observation can trail the newest advance release, even after a successful retrieval. Preserve that limitation in exports. Census retail estimates are nominal, seasonally adjusted and revisable; the latest month is advance. File update and report execution dates are metadata, not evidence of historical publication times.

## Access and advertising

Reuse rights do not waive API limits or authorize bypassing an access denial. Refresh from the official endpoints server-side, respect their limits, and retain last-good observations when requests fail. Do not replace a blocked official feed with an unreviewed mirror.

This review concerns statistical data and calendar reuse rights. Google advertising setup, its policies and privacy/consent implementation remain separate launch work; this calendar change adds no advertising scripts.
