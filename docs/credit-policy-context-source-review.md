# Banking, lending survey and Federal Reserve funding context

Reviewed 2026-10-22. This admission covers ten statistical measurements and factual FOMC calendar/document references. It does not authorize unrelated Federal Reserve content, marks, underlying bank reports, counterparty transaction details or third-party data. No credential, paid service or additional infrastructure is used.

The companion [household-credit review](household-credit-source-review.md) admits five additional DSR and bank-delinquency ratios through the same bounded implementation, with separate endpoint, methodology and population contracts.

## Rights and official access

The [Board copyright policy](https://www.federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm) states that its information is public domain unless otherwise indicated, permits copying/distribution and requests Board attribution. The selected Board-authored H.8, H.4.1 and SLOOS statistical series carry no contrary third-party notice in their program pages or downloaded metadata. Commercial display and raw-value exports are admitted on that scoped basis, with exact program credits, units and calculation labels. The review does not infer rights from HTTP success or extend the policy to Board seals/logos or non-Board materials.

The [New York Fed Terms of Use](https://www.newyorkfed.org/privacy/termsofuse) grant conditional personal and business copying, storage, distribution and derivatives. ON RRP data are **conditionally reusable, not public domain**. Preserve the original **Reverse Repo Operations** title; source and free-source links; the copyright notice for the actual retrieval year; downstream same-permissions/conditions/restrictions notice; no-affiliation/no-endorsement language; and clear Meridian unit-conversion labels. These are stored per series and included in the page and CSV. The separate SOFR/EFFR reference-rate disclaimer is not misrepresented as an ON RRP program condition.

All exact endpoints below returned HTTP 200 during independent live collection. Redirects are rejected; each captured response retains its SHA-256, URL and retrieval instant. Download accessibility is a separate fact from the rights review.

## Exact measurements and treatment

| Meridian ID | Exact source identifier | Admitted meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bankLoans | B1020NCBA | All-commercial-bank loans and leases in bank credit; weekly, seasonally adjusted, outstanding Wednesday stock |
| bankDeposits | B1058NCBA | All-commercial-bank deposits; weekly, seasonally adjusted, outstanding Wednesday stock |
| ciStandardsLarge | SUBLPDCILS_N.Q | Net percentage of domestic-bank respondents tightening C&I standards to large and middle-market firms |
| ciStandardsSmall | SUBLPDCISS_N.Q | Same standards concept, small firms |
| ciDemandLarge | SUBLPDCILD_N.Q | Net percentage of domestic-bank respondents reporting stronger C&I demand from large and middle-market firms |
| ciDemandSmall | SUBLPDCISD_N.Q | Same demand concept, small firms |
| fedAssets | RESPPMA_N.WW | Total assets **less eliminations from consolidation**, all Federal Reserve Banks; Wednesday level |
| reserveBalances | RESH4R_N.WW | Reserve balances with Federal Reserve Banks; Wednesday level |
| tgaBalance | RESPPLLDT_N.WW | Treasury General Account deposits at Federal Reserve Banks; Wednesday level |
| onRrpAccepted | RRP:regular-overnight:totalAmtAccepted | Accepted trade amount in the regular fixed-rate Treasury overnight reverse-repo operation; source operation date |

H.8/H.4.1 source currency values are USD millions (`UNIT_MULT=1000000`), divided by 1000 for USD billions. Exact metadata checks reject different populations, units, frequencies, adjustment or H.4.1 weekly-average/change/district series. H.8 uses the all-commercial-bank population, which includes the program's foreign-related institutions in the United States. It is not the domestic-bank SLOOS response panel. The [H.8 methodology](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h8/about.htm) explains estimation, benchmarking, panel and accounting changes. Level histories can revise; a raw difference is not silently called the Board's published break-adjusted growth rate. No new-lending or deposit-flow series is constructed from stocks.

SLOOS stays in published net percentage units, including negatives and zeros. Standards equal tighter minus easier response shares; demand equals stronger minus weaker shares. These are respondent balances, not changes in outstanding loan volume. Positive standards mean net tightening; positive demand means net stronger demand. A zero does not mean every respondent reported no change. The [program description](https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/sloos/about.htm) describes the survey panel and reference questions. The XML uses quarter-end dates (for example `2026-09-30`); the date is preserved as a **source quarter label**, not a measurement taken on that date, a full-quarter flow, or an original publication date. No projection of that quarterly response into monthly values is made. `comparisonClass: balance` disallows ratio indexing even if a selected window happens to be positive.

SLOOS date handling was corrected after review found that filtering a quarter-end source label against retrieval date discarded already released survey rounds. The [July 2026 survey](https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/sloos/sloos-202607.htm) was released August 3 while its XML series uses the `2026-09-30` label. New captures retain that exact raw value as observation `sourcePeriodLabel` and use `2026-07-01` as the canonical **quarter-start label** for ordering and filtering, with captured series `dateConvention: source-quarter-start-label`. Neither date is the actual survey date, publication date or the end of an observed full-quarter flow. `publishedAt` remains null; no historical availability is inferred from the canonical label. The XML download and retrieval clocks remain separate.

The correction applies only to newly collected records. Older immutable captures retain their original quarter-end row dates and stored metadata. Admission accepts those legacy records when their captured convention is absent or `source-quarter-end-label`; it requires every newly marked quarter-start row to retain a valid matching quarter-end `sourcePeriodLabel`. Missing, malformed or wrong-quarter raw labels fail. Dedicated CSV exports both the canonical date and original source label plus the captured convention; frozen historical packages retain their original fields. No existing archive blob or capture key is rewritten to hide the date-convention change.

ON RRP raw accepted amounts are whole USD dollars, divided by one billion. Results marked as small-value exercises/operational readiness tests in source notes are excluded before daily selection. One regular result per operation date is required; duplicate regular operations fail instead of being added. An overnight term can span weekends/holidays: maturity minus settlement must equal `termCalenderDays` (source spelling), retained as `termCalendarDays`. All selected historical regular operations close at 13:15 Eastern; the observed source opening field is 12:45 except two source records at 12:44 (2024-07-12 and 2025-07-07). Both observed forms are retained. Other windows fail for review rather than being assumed regular. The [ON RRP FAQ](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/rrp_faq.html) describes the operation and reserve/liability accounting. Accepted trade amounts can differ from settled amounts: the source note for 2026-09-21 states a settlement difference and is retained. The accepted-operation series is not the total H.4.1 reverse-repo liability stock and is not added to or subtracted from Wednesday stocks.

## Clocks, history and access limits

The Board histories begin at 2000 where the selected source series exist; H.4.1 begins 2002-12-18. ON RRP begins 2024. These are latest revised downloads, not original-publication vintages. No missing dates are generated, interpolated or carried forward. No historical publication instant is inferred from XML `Prepared`, source `lastUpdated`, observation dates, regular schedules or retrieval. All numerical observation `publishedAt` values remain null. Source values, operation/settlement/maturity metadata and notes survive exports.

Board ZIP responses are bounded to 16 MiB compressed and 192 MiB for the exact XML member, with archive validation/CRC and no filesystem extraction. New formats, unknown observation statuses, malformed values, changed metadata and oversized responses fail for review. The [program pages](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h8/) announce removal of the DDP Build Your Package feature in November 2026 ahead of DDP retirement, while linking release XML downloads. This collector uses the currently linked XML, not the retiring query builder. A later endpoint removal would be a visible source failure; FRED is not silently substituted and no provider rights are inferred from its availability.

The routine site refresh remains an end-of-day capture target, not a promise to ingest H.8/H.4.1/SLOOS/ON RRP immediately at publication. Per-source failures preserve existing retained captures through the shared refresh path; the collectors never overwrite published/archive files themselves. Each new source is independent so one failure need not suppress the others.
